Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

DealsParty4U does not operate a website or app that collects accounts, passwords, or personal profiles from the public. This policy covers the limited data involved in running our deal-posting channels.

What we collect

  • Click data. Deal links are routed through a short redirect (/go/<id>) so we can see how many times a link was clicked and from which platform. We do not collect names, emails, or any other personal information through this redirect.
  • Public platform interactions. Likes, comments, follows, and views on Telegram, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube are visible to us only in the aggregated form each platform provides to channel/account owners — governed by that platform's own privacy policy, not ours.

What we don't do

We don't sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for advertising. We don't require you to create an account or provide personal information to view or click our posts.

Third-party platforms

When you use Telegram, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, or YouTube to view our content, that platform's own privacy policy governs how your data is handled on their service.

Affiliate tracking

Retailers we link to (e.g. Amazon) may use their own cookies or tracking once you leave our redirect link and land on their site. That tracking is governed by the retailer's own privacy policy.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above will reflect the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us on Telegram.
